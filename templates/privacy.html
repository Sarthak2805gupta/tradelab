Privacy & Terms

Plain-English summary of what we collect, how we use it, and the limits of what TradeLab does.

Last updated

April 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: email, username, password (hashed, never stored in plain text). Optionally, your OAuth provider's display name + avatar URL if you sign in with Google/Apple/GitHub.
  • Learning progress: which modules you've completed and quiz scores — linked to your account.
  • Portfolio & watchlist entries: the tickers you add, so we can show them back to you.
  • Anonymous logs: standard server logs (IP, timestamps, URLs visited) for debugging and abuse prevention. Rotated regularly.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data. Ever.
  • We don't share it with advertisers or third-party analytics.
  • We don't track you across the web.
  • We don't hold any of your money or assets — we're not a broker, not a custodian, not an advisor.

Data sources

Price, volume, and fundamentals come from yfinance (an unofficial wrapper around Yahoo Finance). Data can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. We cache aggressively to reduce load — a quote you see may be up to 60 seconds stale on the dashboard, up to 5 minutes stale on scans.

This is not investment advice

Every indicator, rating, and verdict on TradeLab is generated by deterministic rules operating on public data — there is no human analyst behind them. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. We are not registered with SEBI as a Research Analyst or Investment Advisor, and TradeLab is not a substitute for professional financial advice. If you're making real money decisions, consult a SEBI-registered advisor.

Your rights

  • Delete your account: go to the admin or email the operator and your account + all linked data will be deleted within 7 days.
  • Export your data: request a JSON dump of your account, portfolio, watchlist, and learning progress.
  • Correct anything: we'll fix wrong data on request.

Cookies

We set a session cookie when you sign in (standard Django auth) and store your chosen theme (dark/light) in localStorage. No ad cookies, no trackers.

Changes to this page

If we materially change what we do with data, we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date.
