Engine room

Built from scratch

Django 4.2 Python 3.9 yfinance pandas · numpy Tailwind CSS Custom Canvas Charts ThreadPoolExecutor SSE streaming MySQL

All technical indicators (SMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, ATR, ADX, Supertrend) are computed from scratch in pure pandas/numpy — no TA libraries. Dashboard quotes and top movers fetch in a thread pool to keep page loads fast even on a cold cache. Charts are drawn natively on HTML5 canvas — no external chart library.

Disclaimer

This is not investment advice

TradeLab is a learning and research tool. Every rating, verdict, and recommendation is generated by deterministic rules on public market data — there is no human analyst behind them.

Volatility-based risk estimates assume markets stay "normal" and will understate losses in a real crash. Historical patterns don't guarantee future outcomes.

Always do your own research, understand what you're buying, and never invest money you can't afford to lose. If in doubt, speak to a SEBI-registered advisor.
