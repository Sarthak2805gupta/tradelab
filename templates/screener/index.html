How the Screener works

A plain-English walkthrough of the four tabs and what they're for.

What is this?

The Screener scans Indian equities (Nifty 50/100/500) and ranks them using a multi-factor score — trend, momentum, volume, volatility and fundamentals — then lets you backtest strategies and run portfolio tools, all in one place.

The four tabs

  • Ranked Picks Scan an index and get a table of top-scoring stocks. Click Configure to tweak the scoring weights and filters.
  • Backtester Pick a ticker and compare nine different trading strategies on historical data — wins, drawdowns, Sharpe, all of it.
  • Tools Portfolio analysis, position sizing, correlation checks, and other utilities.

Typical flow

  1. Start on Ranked Picks. Choose an index, hit Run Screener, and let it scan (30–90s).
  2. Shortlist a couple of stocks from the table — click a row to see the breakdown.
  3. Switch to Backtester, paste a shortlisted ticker, and see which strategy has historically worked best.

Tips

  • Scoring is fully customizable under ⚙ Configure — raise weights for the factors you trust most.
  • The Fundamental gate and Weekly MTF gate filter out weak candidates before they're ranked.
  • Click ↓ CSV after a scan to export results for your own analysis.
  • Nothing here is investment advice — it's a research aid.
