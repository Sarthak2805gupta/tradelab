How this works

  1. You enter your capital and the maximum 1-month loss you'd accept.
  2. We pull the last 6 months of daily prices for ~25 large-cap NSE stocks and compute:
    • Estimated 1-month downside = recent daily volatility × √21 × 1.65σ (≈ 5%-tail loss).
    • Trend score from the same SMA / RSI / MACD stack used in the analysis page.
  3. We filter to stocks where the downside fits your budget AND at least one share is affordable.
  4. We rank survivors by a fit score that rewards trend strength and risk headroom, and show you the top picks.

Important: this is an objective sizing tool, not investment advice. Volatility-based risk estimates assume markets stay "normal" — they will understate losses in a real crash.
