How this works

We ran {{ result.sims }} simulated futures, each using Nifty's historical long-term averages (~12% annual return, 18% annual volatility). In every simulated month, your SIP contribution goes in and gets multiplied by a random monthly return drawn from that distribution. After {{ result.years }} years × 12 months, we record the final portfolio value. The three numbers above are the bottom-5%, middle, and top-5% outcomes.

This assumes long-term averages hold. Real markets can have decade-long deviations. It's a plausibility tool, not a guarantee.
